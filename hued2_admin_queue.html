Content Review Queue
Open a submission, check the full content, then make the call. Every decision is logged and the creator is emailed.
What Changed From v1
  • Approve and Reject moved off the cards. Decisions happen in the detail view, after seeing the actual content.
  • Detail view shows the full submission per format: video player, social link preview with link health, full article text.
  • The two checks are reviewer checkboxes. Approve stays disabled until both are checked.
  • Reviewers can fix the category before approving. The original category is kept and shown.
  • Reject requires a reason (note required for “Other”) and previews what the creator will see.
  • New Unpublished tab and an Unpublish action on approved items, for takedown and DMCA requests.
  • Tab counts come from one source, so they always match. Stats row removed.
  • Shows rights confirmation, submitter history, and an audit log of who decided what.
  • Reviewer name is set at unlock and shown in the header (replaces hard-coded name).

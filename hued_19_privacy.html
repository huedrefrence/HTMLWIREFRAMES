Privacy Policy
Last updated: [date]

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hued collects when you use hued.info, how we use it, and the choices you have. Hued is operated by Huemanite LLC.

What we collect

Account information. Your username, email, password, role, and the fashion categories you select.

Content you submit. Anything you post to the Knowledge Hub or Brand Threads, including its title, description, and category.

Purchase-related information for Brand Threads. If you upload a purchase confirmation to start a thread, we extract product details, brand, category, size. The image itself is not stored or shown publicly after that.

Activity on the platform. Citations, upvotes, comments, and which categories you engage with.

Basic device information. Standard technical data like browser type and pages visited.

How we use it
  • To run your account and keep it secure
  • To show your submitted content and let others cite or comment on it
  • To notify you when your content is cited, approved, or rejected
  • To create aggregated, anonymous insights we share with brand partners, never your individual data
  • To keep the platform safe and enforce our Terms
What we share, and what we don't

We never sell your personal information. What we do share with brand partners is aggregated and de-identified, category trends, citation patterns, general sentiment, never anything that identifies you individually.

Some of what you post is naturally visible to other users, your username, your public submissions, your public comments. Anything you don't want visible publicly shouldn't be posted.

Your choices

You can update your account information anytime from your profile. You can request that we delete your account by contacting us below.

Children's privacy

Hued is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Hued after a change means you accept the update.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@hued.info.

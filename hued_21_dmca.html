Hued respects intellectual property rights and expects users to do the same. If you believe content on hued.info infringes your copyright, here's how to report it.

Filing a takedown notice

Send a written notice to hello@hued.info that includes:

  • Your name and contact information
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe was infringed
  • The specific URL or location on Hued where the content appears
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized
  • A statement that the information is accurate, and that you're authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner
  • Your physical or electronic signature
What happens next

We'll review valid notices and remove or disable access to the reported content. We'll also notify the user who posted it.

Counter-notification

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, you can submit a counter-notice with your contact information, identification of the removed content, and a statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was a mistake. We'll forward this to the original reporting party.

Repeat infringers

Accounts with repeated, verified copyright violations may be suspended or terminated.

Contact us

Send DMCA notices to hello@hued.info.
